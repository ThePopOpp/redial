How it works

Three minutes between you and quiet.

01
Connect your number
Sign in with Twilio — or Telnyx, Vonage, any SIP line — and ReDial routes its incoming calls through the screen. Keep your own mobile number with one forwarding code.
02
Pick a voice
Bring your own agent — Space xAI, OpenAI Realtime, ElevenLabs, Vapi, Retell — or any endpoint that speaks audio in, audio out. ReDial hands it the call and takes back the transcript.
03
Decide who gets through
Spam only, unknown callers, or every call. Saved contacts always ring you. Urgent callers get connected live. Everyone else leaves a message you read later.
