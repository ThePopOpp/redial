# Phone-first reveal trial

The subsequent [complete card reveal](COMPLETE-CARD-REVEAL.md) extends this trial to all eight cards and replaces the screening word's mask with a fully readable placement. This document records the earlier trial.

The latest visual trial changes only the incoming and screening chapters. The previous sources and relevant tests were copied with SHA-256 hashes to `evidence/pre-staged-reveal-source/` before edits. This supersedes the immediate card-growth behavior for the first two chapters described in `INTERACTIVE-STORY.md`. The background-word experiment remains limited to SCREEN in the second chapter.

Each chapter initially holds a small card, approximately 267px wide on a 1440px desktop. The device stays fully visible while its presentation plays. The first chapter's material/outline/material pass now finishes within the first 38% of the chapter. From 38–62%, the card grows to approximately 499px, rises 27svh, and builds a violet edge glow and soft halo. From 40–62%, the phone enlarges slightly and fades to transparent. Its case, bezel, tracing, screen and shadow share the same opacity curve; the atmospheric scene remains. The card holds for reading before fading out, and the device reforms for the next chapter.

SCREEN starts with crisp, luminous lettering and a separate diffuse bloom. After its initial hold, scrolling enlarges and slides the word, increases blur to 22px, and fades it away. A stationary feathered mask keeps the desktop explanation readable without placing a panel over the type. Light mode uses violet lettering and glow. All reveals derive from scroll position, so reverse scrolling restores the small card, complete phone and sharp word.

The second card unfolds its audio controls as it grows. Hidden controls remain inert, and explicit playback begins with the sample greeting. Manual playback/seeking cancels any outstanding scroll-settle operation so it cannot unexpectedly jump over the greeting. Nothing plays before opt-in. Existing Insider audio, transcript scrubbing, three Audible choices, later card/phone sequences, fixed navigation, theme switch, and onboarding remain available. Reduced motion retains the existing inline controls without the new transformations. Mobile uses smaller growth and travel, including a compact short-screen layout.
